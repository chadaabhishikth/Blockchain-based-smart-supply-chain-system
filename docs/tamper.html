The Oracle Problem (Presentation Talking Point)

Blockchain mathematically guarantees the integrity of digital records after insertion. However, it cannot prevent physical counterfeit tags from being placed on fake items at the point of origin. Therefore, a production-grade supply chain requires IoT RFID sensors and physical inspection paired with the cryptographic ledger.
